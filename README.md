### About

Founder & Chief Executive Officer at **[GID Studio (SMC-Private) Limited](https://gidstudio.com)**, architecting multi-tenant SaaS platforms, cross-platform mobile software, and self-hosted Linux cloud & mail infrastructure from Chowk Azam, Pakistan.

- **Systems & Infrastructure:** Operating dedicated Ubuntu & Arch Linux environments with custom Nginx, Exim, Dovecot, Docker, and DNS/deliverability pipelines.
- **Product Engineering:** Shipping end-to-end ERP, ISP RADIUS billing, POS, and Android utility applications—previously scaled **Campus Bite** as Co-Founder & CTO to **~200 daily active users** and **~500 daily transactions**.
- **Applied AI & Low-Level Tooling:** Running quantized local LLMs (`Ollama` / `LM Studio`), building kernel inode-mapped network telemetry in C/Python, and exploring offensive/defensive cybersecurity.
- **Open Source:** Merged runtime/container improvements upstream in [`coollabsio/jean`](https://github.com/coollabsio/jean) (`#900`).
